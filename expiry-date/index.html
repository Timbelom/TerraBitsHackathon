How to read this

Each row is one site. Each square is one possible launch delay (day 0 = your landing date). A square is green when, during the whole stay, the Sun is never below half-visible for longer than the Sun limit, and the Earth never for longer than the Earth limit. The label counts the green squares from day 0 until the first red one.

Sites with terrain data show a range (horizon ±1 σ of the LOLA height uncertainty). Sites without terrain data on the polar cap only show an upper bound, because mountains can only make things worse.
