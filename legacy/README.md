# legacy

Earlier work of the project, kept for reference: the stand-alone 3D viewer (`global_viewer.py`, `slice.py` and the two pages they wrote), the pages `globe.html`, `terrain.html` and `explore.html` with their scripts, style sheets and data files, an unused normal map, and the settings and schema of the planning board (`config.js`, `schema.sql`). None of it is part of the product; the page (`index.html`) loads nothing from this folder, and the files are not maintained and may no longer run from here.
