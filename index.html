    01 / THE VALUE

    FROM LUNAR DATA TO REAL DECISIONS

    Explore the terrain.
    Plan the mission.

    A lunar landing site is more than a set of coordinates. L.U.N.A. lets explorers, researchers and mission planners see how terrain and time affect illumination and communication with Earth.

    • Find viable locations.Compare topography, illumination and candidate sites in an interactive 3D view.
    • Plan around illumination.Step through time to study changing illumination, shadows and periods of darkness without solar power.
    • Assess DTE communication.Examine terrain-dependent Earth visibility and outages of the direct-to-Earth (DTE) line of sight.
    • Make complex data understandable.Bring models, time-dependent calculations and visual layers into one explorable view.

    02 / OUR MISSION

    One Moon.
    Every perspective.

    L.U.N.A. brings lunar exploration into one flexible 3D workspace. Navigate the entire Moon, view terrain at true scale, explore coordinates and landing sites, and switch between relief, imagery, elevation and analytical overlays.

    Move through time to see how illumination, shadows and the DTE line of sight change, then assess the conditions that matter for exploration. Built on NASA LOLA topography and JPL Horizons ephemerides.

    An accessible bridge between scientific datasets, visual understanding and better-informed mission planning.
